# HR Analytics: Employee Satisfaction & Retention

## 📌 Problem
Salt Corporation is losing employees. Many are leaving the company.

## 🎯 Objective
To understand why employees are leaving and what can be improved.

## 📊 Dataset
- Data was provided by the DataCamp organization that trained me during the program. This data was for public use.
- 1,000 employees
- 24 columns
- Year: 2024

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PowerPoint

## 🔍 What I Did
- Cleaned messy data (missing values, wrong formats)
- Analyzed satisfaction and attrition
- Created charts and insights

## 📈 Key Insights
- 39.2% of employees left the company
- Employees with low satisfaction leave more
- Finance has low satisfaction
- Marketing has highest attrition
- Only 42.9% use company benefits

## 💡 Recommendations
- Improve employee satisfaction
- Focus on Finance and Marketing departments
- Increase benefits awareness
- Improve company culture

## 📎 Files
- raw_data.xlsx, cleaned_data.xlsx, presentation.pptx

## 🚀 Conclusion
Low satisfaction is strongly associated with attrition. Further analysis would be needed to confirm cause.
